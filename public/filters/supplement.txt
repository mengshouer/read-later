! Title: Read Later built-in rules
! Description: Only the rules the subscribed upstream lists do not already carry, or ship in a form a uBO-compatible engine discards. Every line was checked against AdGuard's URL Tracking Protection filter — none of it is here because it seemed like a good idea.
! Expires: 30 days

! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Params AdGuard's list ships in a form uBO throws away.
!
! It writes them as `$denyallow=<sites>,removeparam=<param>` with no `$domain`,
! and uBO voids the entire filter in that case:
!
!   case NODE_TYPE_NET_OPTION_NAME_DENYALLOW:
!       realBad = isNegated || hasValue === false ||
!           this.getBranchFromType(NODE_TYPE_NET_OPTION_NAME_FROM) === 0;
!
! Same code in the current uBO master and in the engine `scripts/filter-probe.mjs`
! diffs against, and measured both ways: with `$domain` the rule applies, without it
! nothing happens. 21 rules are affected and the six canonical `utm_*` params are
! among them — so subscribing to that list does NOT strip utm_source.
!
! Restated below as plain rules, which is the uBO-valid way to say the same thing.
!
! How much of AdGuard's per-site sparing survives that, measured rather than assumed:
! the 21 rules name 268 host-param pairs, and only 70 of those also have a matching `@@`
! exception in the list. The exceptions that DO exist are valid, we honour them, and since
! every list compiles into one pool they go on protecting those sites. The rest do not
! survive — 193 of the 198 are `utm_campaign` on the `google.*` ccTLDs, where AdGuard
! presumably spares it to avoid breaking a redirect. For a dedup key that is harmless and
! arguably wanted: two google URLs differing only in `utm_campaign` SHOULD be one entry.
! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
$removeparam=utm_source
$removeparam=utm_medium
$removeparam=utm_campaign
$removeparam=utm_content
$removeparam=utm_term
$removeparam=utm_referrer
$removeparam=_ga
$removeparam=cmpid
$removeparam=cuid
$removeparam=a8
$removeparam=adj_t
$removeparam=irclickid
$removeparam=tduid
$removeparam=erid
$removeparam=vc_lpp
$removeparam=oly_enc_id
$removeparam=elq
$removeparam=elqaid
$removeparam=elqat
$removeparam=elqak
$removeparam=elqTrackId
